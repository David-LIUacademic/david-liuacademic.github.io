Hello, and welcome to my academic homepage. I’m **Xuan Liu**, a PhD student in Knowledge Science in the **Shirahada Lab** at the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST) and a JST-supported JAIST-SPRING Researcher.

My long-term ambition is to become an **orchestrator who helps build wiser circular service ecosystems**. To me, orchestration means helping firms, public authorities, and communities bring their knowledge and resources together and coordinate decisions. A wiser ecosystem learns from what happens in practice and adapts as conditions change, so its participants can keep resources in use and sustain circular practices over time.

**Fieldwork and industry partnerships.** I study how this works in practice through interviews and fieldwork in China and Japan. Our research team works with small, medium-sized, and large private firms in both countries, as well as several Chinese state-owned enterprises. These partnerships let me compare how firms of different sizes and ownership structures approach circular transitions across sectors such as metal and textile processing, pollution control technology, fossil fuel processing, and automotive components.

- **Manufacturing firms.** I study how leaders shape organizational culture and build the capabilities firms need to put circular practices into action and sustain them through servitization and digitalization.
- **B2B value chains.** I study how digital tools help buyers check whether an industrial solution is working and when they need their suppliers’ expertise to adapt it. I examine how shared data and specialized know-how shape firms’ ability to sustain circular practices as requirements change.
- **Circular service ecosystems.** I examine how firms, public authorities, and other local actors coordinate circular practices. My research includes case studies in eco-industrial parks in Nanjing and Jiangyin, Jiangsu Province, China, and exploratory fieldwork in Gamagori, Aichi Prefecture, Japan.

**International research collaboration.** Through **xREGEN**, I work with Dr Kaja Antlej at Deakin University in Australia and Associate Professor Dejan Križaj at the University of Primorska in Slovenia to study how circular governance can support community recovery and regeneration. My wider professional network includes researchers and industry practitioners in the Netherlands, the UK, and the US.

I’m a member of **ServCollab**, an international nonprofit that brings service researchers together to improve well-being. I also serve on the student organizing committee of Japan’s Society for Serviceology and am a student member of the Service Science Society of Taiwan. Our study on servitization and the circular economy received the **Best Theme Paper Award at ICSSI 2024**.

I welcome collaborations that build on my fieldwork and industry relationships in China and Japan. I’m especially interested in pairing case studies with quantitative methods to test which practices support circular transitions across different types of firms and assess their effects on operations and the environment.
